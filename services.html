What We Do

Custom Woodworking Services in San Antonio

Lone Star Live Edge is a full-service woodworking studio specializing in handcrafted furniture made from Texas hardwoods. From large dining tables to refinishing heirloom pieces, we combine traditional craftsmanship with modern design.

Custom Live Edge Furniture

We design and build custom dining tables, coffee tables, desks, conference tables, and statement furniture. Each project begins with selecting the right slab, followed by careful design, joinery, and finishing.

Materials

Wood Species Guide

We specialize in Texas hardwoods known for their beauty, durability, and distinctive grain patterns.

Mesquite

Mesquite

Extremely stable hardwood known for dramatic grain. One of the most sought-after woods for live edge furniture.

Pecan

Pecan

Strong, warm colored wood perfect for large tables. Features unique grain patterns and warm tones.

Cedar Elm

Cedar Elm

Rustic texture and excellent durability. Works beautifully in both rustic and modern interiors.

Texas Walnut

Texas Walnut

Rich dark tones ideal for modern interiors. Pairs especially well with modern metal bases.

Black Walnut

Black Walnut

One of the most sought-after furniture woods in the world. Elegant dark grain with a luxurious finish.
