Our Story

Crafted From Texas Timber. Built to Last Generations.

Lone Star Live Edge is a veteran-owned woodworking studio in San Antonio, Texas specializing in handcrafted live edge furniture made from locally sourced hardwood.

Owner Rob Faulkner brings the same discipline and precision learned during his service in the U.S. Navy to every project that enters the shop. Each slab is carefully selected, kiln-dried, and transformed into heirloom furniture using traditional woodworking techniques.

We work primarily with Texas hardwood species such as mesquite, pecan, cedar elm, and walnut — woods prized for their durability and striking grain patterns. Every table, desk, or mantel is truly one-of-a-kind.
