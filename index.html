Who We Are

Real Wood. Real Craft. Real San Antonio.

Alamo Live Edge Furniture Co. is the workshop of Rob Faulkner — a Navy veteran who traded steel decks for sawdust floors. We build custom live edge furniture from Texas hardwoods, one slab at a time, right here in San Antonio. No factory lines. No mass production. Just honest woodwork and a handshake you can count on.
