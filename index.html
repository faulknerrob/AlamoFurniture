Who We Are

Real Wood. Real Craft. Real San Antonio.

LoneStar Woodworking & Live Edge Furniture Co. is the workshop of Rob Faulkner — a Navy veteran who traded steel decks for sawdust floors. We build custom live edge furniture from Texas hardwoods, one slab at a time, right here in San Antonio. No factory lines. No mass production. Just honest woodwork and a handshake you can count on.

From Raw Slab to Finished Table

See the Transformation

Every live edge table starts as a raw slab — rough, unfinished, and full of potential. Here's what happens when we get our hands on it.

Before

A raw walnut slab — rough-cut, bark still on, full of natural character. Knots, grain patterns, and live edges waiting to be revealed.

After

Sanded smooth, filled, sealed, and mounted on custom steel hairpin legs. A one-of-a-kind dining table ready for generations of family meals.
