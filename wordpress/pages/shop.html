How It Works

From First Conversation to Final Delivery

Building custom furniture shouldn't be complicated. Here's how we keep it simple.

1

Consult

Tell us about your space, your style, and what you're looking for. We'll talk through wood options, dimensions, and design ideas. No pressure, no obligation.

2

Design

We'll pick the perfect slab, finalize the design details — legs, finish, edge treatment — and give you a clear quote with no surprise costs.

3

Build

Your piece gets built by hand in our San Antonio workshop. We'll send progress photos along the way so you can watch it come together.

4

Deliver

We deliver to your home or business anywhere in the San Antonio metro and surrounding Hill Country area. Setup and placement included.
